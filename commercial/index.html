Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Wenatchee

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Wenatchee HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Wenatchee and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems.
